The E-Waste Crisis

📱

Short Product Life Cycles

Electronic devices are designed with planned obsolescence, leading to frequent replacements and massive waste generation.

🗑️

Mounting E-Waste

Millions of tons of electronic waste are generated annually, with only a small percentage being properly recycled or refurbished.

💰

High Ownership Costs

Consumers face high upfront costs for technology purchases, making quality devices inaccessible to many.

🌍

Environmental Impact

E-waste contributes to environmental pollution and resource depletion, affecting our planet's sustainability.

How SCSC Works

1. Subscribe

Choose from our Basic, Standard, or Premium plans based on your needs and budget.

2. Use

Receive a high-quality, refurbished device and use it for your subscription period.

3. Return

Return your device when upgrading or at the end of your subscription term.

4. Refurbish

Our certified technicians refurbish returned devices to like-new condition.
