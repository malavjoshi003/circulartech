OUR PURPOSE

More useful life.
Less unnecessary waste.

CircularTech explores how access, repair, and reuse can support more thoughtful technology consumption.

A CIRCULAR APPROACH

Keep technology useful for longer.

Our concept considers the entire device journey, from subscription to return and refurbishment.

01

Extend device life

Prioritize maintenance and suitable repairs so working electronics can remain useful for longer.

02

Support reuse

Give returned devices a route back into service through inspection, refurbishment, and reassignment.

03

Improve access

Explore subscription options that reduce the upfront commitment required to access a device.

MEASURE WHAT MATTERS

Impact should be measured.

This student project has no live operational impact data. A future implementation could track device returns, refurbishment outcomes, and resource use to assess its results.

Proposed indicators

  • Devices returned after subscriptions
  • Devices successfully refurbished
  • Additional useful months per device
  • Components routed to appropriate recyclers
  • Repair and refurbishment turnaround time
PEOPLE & OPPORTUNITY

Consider the people behind the devices.

Technology access

A subscription model could offer students, freelancers, and small businesses another way to access technology without purchasing a device outright.

Repair and refurbishment skills

A working circular service would need inspection, repair, testing, and logistics skills to prepare devices for their next users.
