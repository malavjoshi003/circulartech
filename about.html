Our Story

📚

Innovation in Start-ups Project

The Subscription-Based Circular Supply Chain (SCSC) project was developed as part of the "Innovation in Start-ups" elective course at Navrachana University. This project represents our commitment to addressing real-world challenges through innovative business solutions.

Our team recognized the growing e-waste crisis and the need for sustainable technology consumption models. Through extensive research and innovation, we developed SCSC as a comprehensive solution that benefits consumers, businesses, and the environment.

This project demonstrates how academic learning can translate into practical solutions that address pressing global challenges while creating sustainable business opportunities.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To revolutionize technology consumption by implementing a sustainable circular supply chain model that reduces e-waste, makes technology accessible to all income levels, and promotes environmental responsibility while creating profitable business opportunities.

Key Objectives:

  • Reduce electronic waste through refurbishment
  • Make technology accessible to underserved populations
  • Promote sustainable consumption patterns
  • Create innovative business models
  • Contribute to Sustainable Development Goals
🔮

Our Vision

To become a leading force in transforming the technology industry towards sustainability, where circular economy principles are the norm, and technology access is equitable, affordable, and environmentally responsible.

Future Goals:

  • Scale the SCSC model nationally
  • Partner with major technology brands
  • Influence industry sustainability policies
  • Create a replicable global model
  • Lead the circular economy transformation
