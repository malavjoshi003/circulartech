ABOUT CIRCULARTECH

A fresh perspective on technology access.

A student website exploring a subscription-based circular supply chain for electronic devices.

THE IDEA

Access today. Consider tomorrow.

CircularTech brings together device subscriptions, returns, refurbishment, and reuse in one proposed service model.

The goal is to explain how technology access could become more flexible while keeping devices useful for longer.

What this website demonstrates

  • A sustainable device subscription concept
  • Illustrative plans and pricing
  • A proposed circular device lifecycle
  • A responsive, multi-page website

Project scope

CircularTech is an academic demonstration, not an operating subscription company. It does not process payments, manage customer accounts, or arrange device delivery.
