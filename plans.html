A few common questions

Can I subscribe through this website?

This is a student demonstration. Real subscriptions, checkout, and device delivery are not available.

What happens when a device is returned?

The proposed process includes inspection, data erasure, repair where suitable, and reuse or appropriate recycling.

Are damage and cancellation terms included?

Final commercial terms have not been implemented. A real service would need clear damage, cancellation, warranty, and return policies.

Could institutions use this model?

The concept could be adapted for schools or businesses needing access to multiple devices.
