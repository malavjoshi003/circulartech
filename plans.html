Frequently Asked Questions

What are my responsibilities as a subscriber?

Subscribers are responsible for:

  • Using the device as intended and following usage guidelines
  • Reporting any issues or damage promptly
  • Maintaining regular subscription payments
  • Returning the device in good condition at the end of the term
  • Protecting personal data and following security protocols

What happens if my device gets damaged?

Our damage policy covers:

  • Normal wear and tear: Covered under warranty
  • Accidental damage: Repair or replacement at minimal cost
  • Intentional damage: Repair costs borne by subscriber
  • Loss or theft: Replacement device with deductible

Can I cancel my subscription early?

Yes, early cancellation is possible with the following terms:

  • 30-day notice period required
  • Early termination fee may apply
  • Device must be returned in good condition
  • Refund of unused subscription period (minus fees)

How is my data protected?

We prioritize data security through:

  • Complete data wiping before refurbishment
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure storage protocols
  • Compliance with data protection regulations
  • Regular security audits and updates

What warranty coverage do I get?

Warranty coverage varies by plan:

  • Basic: 6-month standard warranty
  • Standard: 12-month extended warranty
  • Premium: 24-month comprehensive warranty
  • All plans include technical support and repair services

How does the upgrade process work?

Upgrading is simple and flexible:

  • Upgrade available after minimum subscription period
  • Return current device in good condition
  • Pay difference in subscription fees
  • Receive upgraded device within 5-7 business days
  • No additional setup fees for upgrades
